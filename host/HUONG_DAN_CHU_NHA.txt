TU THANH CHIEN — HUONG DAN CHU NHA (CHOI QUA MANG LAN)
=======================================================

GOI NAY CO GI:
  server/        Chuong trinh may chu (giu toan bo tran dau):
                   - tu-thanh-chien-win.exe + tu-thanh-chien-win.console.exe
                     + tu-thanh-chien-win.pck   (cho may Windows)
                   - tu-thanh-chien.x86_64 + tu-thanh-chien.pck (cho may Linux)
  web/           Ban game cho trinh duyet (may chu tu phuc vu)
  start_host.bat (Windows) / start_host.sh (Linux)
                 File khoi dong — chi can chay 1 file nay, dung chay
                 thang file trong server/.

=======================================================
A. MAY CHU NHA LA WINDOWS (lam theo thu tu)
=======================================================
  Buoc 1: Giai nen goi (neu nhan dang file nen) roi mo thu muc
          goi-chu-nha. NHAN DUP (double-click) vao file:
              start_host.bat
          - Neu Windows hien hop "Windows protected your PC"
            (SmartScreen — vi chuong trinh chua co chu ky so, binh
            thuong): bam "More info" -> bam "Run anyway".
  Buoc 2: Windows Firewall hoi quyen mang (chi hoi lan dau):
          TICH o "Private networks" (mang rieng) roi bam
          "Allow access". Khong bam thi ban be khong vao duoc.
  Buoc 3: Cua so den hien cac dong "IPv4 Address . . . : 192.168.x.x"
          — do la IP LAN cua may ban. Hay nho so nay.
          (Neu co nhieu dong: chon dong bat dau bang 192.168. hoac
          10. — dong dang 172. la cua may ao/VPN, dung gui dong do.)
  Buoc 4: Ban (chu nha) choi cung: mo trinh duyet tren chinh may
          minh toi:
              http://localhost:8911/?lan=1
          Trong man hinh choi LAN, bam TAO PHONG (dat ten phong neu
          muon). Game se hien MA PHONG gom 6 ky tu — nho ma nay.
  Buoc 5: Gui cho ban be CUNG WIFI voi ban HAI thu:
            (1) duong dan:  http://<IP-o-buoc-3>:8911/?lan=1
                Vi du: http://192.168.1.8:8911/?lan=1
            (2) MA PHONG o Buoc 4.
          Ban be chi can mo bang trinh duyet (Chrome/Edge/Firefox),
          KHONG can cai gi them, KHONG can tai khoan.
  Buoc 6: Ban be mo duong dan -> nhap TEN HIEN THI + MA PHONG ->
          bam VAO PHONG. Sai ma thi game bao loi va khong cho vao.
  Buoc 7: O phong cho, chu phong thay danh sach 4 slot. Bam nut
          CHE DO o slot trong de doi: CHO NGUOI (ai co ma vao duoc,
          luc bat dau con trong thi BOT lap) / BOT (bot giu cho) /
          DONG (phe do khong tham gia tran). Khi moi nguoi da vao,
          chu phong bam BAT DAU TRAN (khong nhat thiet cho moi nguoi
          bam SAN SANG — nut do chi de bao hieu).
  Luu y: GIU cua so den cua start_host.bat mo suot tran —
         tat cua so = may chu tat = tran dung.

=======================================================
B. MAY CHU NHA LA LINUX
=======================================================
  Mo terminal trong thu muc goi-chu-nha, chay:  ./start_host.sh
  Cua so in san duong dan co IP LAN de gui ban be.
  Cac buoc vao phong (4-7 o tren) giong het Windows.

=======================================================
LUU Y CHUNG
=======================================================
  - Dung tat cua so may chu giua tran: may chu tat = tran dung.
  - Ai rot mang giua tran: vao lai cung duong dan trong vong 60 giay
    se duoc tra lai dung phe cua minh (game tu nho bang token).
    Qua 60 giay, bot se tiep quan phe do.
  - Khong can mo cong router, khong can internet khi dang choi.
  - Neu trinh duyet bao loi "Secure Context" khi mo bang IP LAN:
    mo chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure,
    them http://<IP-cua-ban>:8911 vao o trong, chon Enabled, mo lai
    trinh duyet. (Ban trong goi da duoc va san phan kiem tra nay,
    day chi la du phong.)
  - Ban be mo link ma khong vao duoc: kiem tra (1) cung 1 wifi,
    (2) da bam Allow o Buoc 2 chua, (3) IP go dung chua,
    (4) cua so may chu con mo khong.
